# NorthWind Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes NorthWind sales data using Microsoft Power BI to evaluate business performance, monitor key performance indicators, and uncover insights into sales trends, product categories, customer behavior, supplier performance, and employee productivity.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Evaluate revenue contributions across product categories.
- Identify top-performing products and suppliers.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and revenue contributions.
- Monitor employee performance through orders and revenue.
- Track business KPIs through interactive dashboards.

## Data Preparation & Cleaning
The data preparation process included:
- Reviewed the dataset structure and data types.
- Checked for missing values and duplicate records.
- Validated data consistency across tables.
- Prepared and transformed data for analysis using Power BI.
- Established relationships between relevant tables to support accurate analysis.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
- KPI Development
- Business Performance Analysis

## Key Metrics
- **Total Revenue:** $1.27M
- **Total Orders:** 830
- **Total Products:** 77
- **Total Suppliers:** 29
- **Total Customers:** 91
- **Total Employees:** 9
- **Total Countries:** 21

## Key Insights

- **Revenue Performance:** Generated approximately $1.27M in total revenue across 830 orders.
- **Category Performance:** Beverages was the highest-revenue category, contributing approximately $268K, followed by Dairy Products at $235K.
- **Product Performance:** Raclette Courdavault was the top product by orders, with 54 orders and approximately $71.2K in revenue.
- **Geographical Performance:** Germany recorded the highest number of orders, while the USA generated the highest revenue among countries.
- **Customer Analysis:** QUICK-Stop generated the highest customer revenue at approximately $110.28K, while Save-a-lot Markets recorded the most orders with 31.
- **Employee Performance:** Margaret Peacock was the top-performing employee, recording 156 orders and approximately $232.89K in revenue.
- **Revenue Targets:** Yearly revenue reached $785.08K against a target of $480.72K, exceeding the target by 63.31%.
